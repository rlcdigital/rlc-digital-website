Timesheet Management for Jira Cloud

A time-logging and approval solution built for teams that want Jira-native worklogs without leaving their existing workflow.

Timesheet Management comes in two tiers: LITE, a free calendar-based time-logging tool, and PRO, which adds a full submit, approve, forward, and lock weekly workflow for teams that need governance over their timesheets.

Timesheet Management LITE

  • Calendar week grid — click a day to log time; Report tab exports to CSV/Excel
  • Replaces Jira's native "Log work" action and "Work log" tab when set as the site's Time Tracking Provider
  • No approval workflow — every worklog belongs to its own user
  • Admin controls: block worklogs after issue resolution date; per-user "Needs Correction" tab catches entries affected after the fact
Get LITE on the Atlassian Marketplace

Timesheet Management PRO

  • Everything in LITE, plus a full submit, approve, forward, and lock weekly workflow
  • Four tabs: Calendar, Timesheet (rollup by user/space/work item, exportable, Submit Week), Needs Correction, Approvals
  • Approvals snapshot worklogs at approval time — they survive later edits, and a daily scan reopens approval if the source worklog is deleted
  • Forward: an approver can delegate approve rights for one (person, space, week) slice to someone else
  • Lock: once a week is fully approved site-wide, it can be locked (manual or auto) — one-way, admin-only unlock
